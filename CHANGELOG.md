# Changelog

Each version is one PSA PSGC release, tagged `YYYY-Qn`.

## 2026-Q2: PSA 2Q 2026, as of 30 June 2026

First version of this repository. Compared with PSA's 2Q 2025 release, it includes these updates:

- **2Q 2026:** the Municipality of San Isidro, Davao del Norte is renamed **Sawata**, and its
  Barangay Sawata is renamed **Poblacion** (RA 11814, plebiscite of 11 April 2026). Fifteen names
  were corrected, including Don Victoriano (Misamis Occidental), Tagoloan II (Lanao del Sur) and
  several barangays in Bukidnon.
- **1Q 2026:** Barangay San Rafael, City of Calaca, Batangas (`0401007038`) is **merged into
  Barangay Dacanlao** (`0401007019`) after the Supreme Court decision in G.R. No. 234228. Its
  code is retired. Corrected names include Sanchez Mira (Cagayan), Genitligan (Baras), Ambuklao
  and Daklan (Bokod).
- **4Q 2025:** corrected names Villa Floresta (San Jose City), and Sto. Niño and Sto. Rosario
  (Magallanes, Agusan del Norte).
- **3Q 2025:** corrected names Lubosan (Lapuyan) and Pinagsakayan (Calauag).

Totals: 18 regions, 82 provinces, 149 cities, 1,493 municipalities, 42,010 barangays.
